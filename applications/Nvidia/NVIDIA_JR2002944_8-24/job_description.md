NVIDIA is seeking a Solutions Architect for its AI Software Segment team. The team works at the intersection of research and engineering to guide successful enterprise adoption of NVIDIA AI software across deep learning, generative AI, and cloud environments.

The role involves designing and building enterprise AI solutions, working closely with customers and partners, and solving challenges in customizing and deploying AI workloads at scale.

- Develop end-to-end AI solutions for enterprise use cases and help customers adopt NVIDIA AI models and libraries.
- Address challenges across the AI model lifecycle, including data processing, orchestration, training, post-training, reinforcement learning, evaluation, and model optimization.
- Support models and applications spanning LLMs, multimodal AI, retrieval, speech, content safety, and edge use cases.

- Help improve NVIDIA products and solve scaling challenges across computer architecture, models, libraries, and AI applications.
