## Overview

NVIDIA is hiring Senior Deep Learning Scientists to advance streaming and agentic multimodal AI. The role applies expertise in deep learning, reinforcement learning, and applied mathematics to develop models that reason, plan, and act across modalities.

The work includes core algorithmic improvements for multimodal foundation models and scaling those ideas through the Nemotron Omni and VoiceChat platforms. The team develops high-impact large language models and multimodal AI products for millions of users.

## What You'll Be Doing

- Apply fundamental and applied research to develop, train, fine-tune, and deploy large language models for agentic systems spanning audio-visual reasoning, tool use, and document understanding.
- Advance post-training and alignment through instruction tuning, preference optimization, and RLHF, RLVR, or MOPD for complex multimodal-agent use cases.
- Research and develop agentic reasoning and grounded-perception capabilities, including planning, tool execution, and long-horizon task completion across digital and physical environments.
- Lead the collection, development, and benchmarking of multimodal datasets.
- Ensure high-quality evaluation of model accuracy, safety, and task-completion success.

## What NVIDIA Needs to See

- Master's degree, PhD, or equivalent experience in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, or Applied Mathematics, with eight or more years of relevant work experience.
- Excellent Python programming skills.
- Strong fundamentals in scalable model development and deep-learning frameworks such as PyTorch.
- Strong knowledge of modern foundation-model architectures, including Transformers and mixture-of-experts models.
- Foundational understanding of reinforcement-learning algorithms and implementation, including Markov decision processes, policies, and reward design.
- Hands-on experience post-training multimodal models for omni-modal audio-visual reasoning, full-duplex voice chat, and human-AI interaction.
- Experience managing model-development life cycles, including dataset versioning, experiment tracking, and evaluation pipelines.

## Ways to Stand Out

- A strong publication record in leading AI and machine-learning venues such as NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, or CVPR.
- Experience training and deploying multimodal foundation models using large-scale distributed infrastructure.
- Experience applying deep reinforcement learning to multimodal agents in complex simulation or gaming environments.
- Background in audio or speech AI, including audio language models or audio generation.
- Experience building embodied-AI systems that combine multimodal perception, backend action fulfillment, and long-horizon planning.
