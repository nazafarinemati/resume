## Overview

FlashDreams and FastGen are NVIDIA technologies for turning video models into real-time world simulations. The stack includes model adaptation for faster generation and richer control, plus an execution layer that runs those models as responsive experiences.

The team develops and ships technology for generative worlds that users can explore and direct. Applications include autonomous-driving simulation, robot-policy development and testing, game worlds, medical simulation, and virtual training.

As a Research Engineer, the successful candidate will contribute across model development and runtime systems, build new capabilities, and help turn research into systems that work in real applications. The team collaborates across the world-model ecosystem, from emerging startups to established model laboratories.

## What You'll Be Doing

- Build and optimize a continuous autoregressive serving loop with per-step control inputs, model and KV-cache state management, GPU inference, frame streaming, and model integrations.
- Advance production-ready world models by collaborating with researchers on few-step distillation, causal or autoregressive generation, reward fine-tuning, action conditioning, and long-horizon spatiotemporal memory and consistency.
- Deliver multi-user experiences and simulation workflows from prototype through evaluation, integration, and release.
- Strengthen the open-source platform through testing, CI/CD, observability, documentation, and developer workflows.
- Partner with researchers and users to improve platform reliability and adoption.
