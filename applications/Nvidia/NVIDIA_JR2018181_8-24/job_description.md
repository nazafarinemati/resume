## Overview

NVIDIA's Silicon Co-Design Group is seeking an Applied AI Engineer to develop and integrate AI solutions into the design and automation infrastructure that powers NVIDIA chips. The group is rebuilding its toolchain around AI and is looking for an engineer to architect and implement solutions that improve workflow efficiency, scalability, and intelligence from concept through deployment.

Every NVIDIA CPU, GPU, and Tegra SoC shipped in the past four years passed through this toolchain on its way to production, and more than 200 product SKUs were optimized during the Blackwell generation.

## What You'll Be Doing

- **LLM-Powered Validation Pipelines:** Design and deploy AI systems that make post-silicon validation faster, smarter, and more scalable across semiconductor environments.
- **Cross-Team AI Integration:** Work with cross-functional engineering teams to identify where AI can eliminate friction and build solutions whose impact spans teams, products, and silicon generations.
- **Technology Scouting and Evaluation:** Evaluate emerging AI frameworks and architectures, identify promising technologies, and advocate for their adoption.
- **Impact Measurement and Continuous Improvement:** Build data systems that demonstrate what is working, establish quantitative indicators of AI impact, close performance gaps, and drive continuous iteration.

## What NVIDIA Needs to See

- BS, MS, PhD, or equivalent experience in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
- Five or more years of hands-on experience building and deploying ML/AI systems or data-intensive backend services.
- Two or more years of direct Applied AI experience independently owning an AI agent, LLM-powered workflow, or intelligent automation system end to end—from prototype through production deployment.
- Strong Python skills and proficiency in at least one static language such as C, C++, C#, Java, or Scala.
- Strong electrical-engineering fundamentals, including computer architecture, high-speed interfaces, timing, power basics, firmware/driver structures, and hardware interaction.
- Hands-on experience in production test, system validation, post-silicon bring-up, reliability, silicon debug, or silicon productization areas such as ATE, SLT, board-level test, validation, or yield analysis.
- Experience in a silicon development environment with exposure to chip and system characterization methods.
- Familiarity with manufacturing and quality metrics such as yield, FPY, DPPM, RAS, TTR, and escape rate.
- Ability to balance multiple concurrent projects while demonstrating strong problem-solving, communication, and teamwork.

## Ways to Stand Out

- Exposure to GPU, CPU, AI accelerator, networking, automotive, or other large-scale SoC programs.
- Familiarity with modern AI technologies and methods for creating and launching LLMs.
- Experience building and deploying orchestration agents that manage hundreds to thousands of tools.
- Ability to translate innovative AI research into practical, high-impact production tools.
- Experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Hands-on experience with agentic and orchestration tools such as NeMo Agent Toolkit, LangChain, Semantic Kernel, AutoGen, CrewAI, or n8n.
