## Overview

NVIDIA's Algorithmic Model Optimization Team focuses on maximizing inference efficiency for generative AI models, including large language models and diffusion models. Its techniques range from neural architecture search and pruning to sparsity, quantization, and automated deployment.

The team's work combines applied research in model efficiency with development of the TensorRT Model Optimizer software platform, which is used internally across NVIDIA and externally by research and engineering teams.

NVIDIA is seeking a Senior Deep Learning Software Engineer to develop and scale automated inference and deployment solutions. The work spans high-level frameworks such as PyTorch and Hugging Face, high-performance kernel implementations in CUDA, TensorRT-LLM, and Triton, and the boundary between machine-learning research and software architecture.

## What You'll Be Doing

- Train, develop, and deploy state-of-the-art generative AI models such as LLMs and diffusion models using NVIDIA's AI software stack.
- Build on the PyTorch 2.0 ecosystem—including TorchDynamo, torch.export, and torch.compile—to analyze arbitrary PyTorch models and extract standardized model-graph representations for automated deployment.
- Develop high-performance inference-optimization techniques, including automated model sharding through tensor or sequence parallelism and efficient attention kernels with KV caching.
- Collaborate across NVIDIA to integrate performant kernel implementations into automated deployment solutions.
- Analyze and profile GPU kernel-level performance to identify hardware and software optimization opportunities.
- Continuously improve inference performance across TensorRT, TensorRT-LLM, and TensorRT Model Optimizer.
- Architect and design a modular, scalable software platform with broad model support, strong usability, and optimization techniques that increase adoption.
