## Overview

NVIDIA's Silicon Co-Design Group is seeking an Applied AI Engineer to innovate, develop, and integrate innovative AI solutions into the design and automation infrastructure that powers our chips. Every CPU, GPU, and Tegra SoC NVIDIA has shipped in the past four years passed through our toolchain on its way to production—over 200 product SKUs were optimized during the Blackwell generation alone.

The group is rebuilding that toolchain around AI and is looking for an engineer to architect and implement solutions that enhance the efficiency, scalability, and intelligence of its workflows, driving initiatives from concept to deployment.

## What You'll Be Doing

- **LLM-Powered Validation Pipelines:** Design and deploy AI systems that make post-silicon validation faster, smarter, and more scalable across semiconductor environments.
- **Cross-Team AI Integration:** Work with cross-functional engineering teams to identify where AI can eliminate friction and build solutions whose impact spans teams, products, and silicon generations.
- **Technology Scouting and Evaluation:** Evaluate emerging AI frameworks and architectures, identify promising technologies, and make the case for adoption.
- **Impact Measurement and Continuous Improvement:** Build data systems that demonstrate what is working, establish quantitative indicators of AI impact, close performance gaps, and drive iteration.

## What NVIDIA Needs to See

- BS, MS, PhD, or equivalent experience in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
- Five or more years of hands-on experience building and deploying ML/AI systems or data-intensive backend services.
- Two or more years of direct Applied AI experience independently owning an AI agent, LLM-powered workflow, or intelligent automation system end to end—from prototype through production deployment.
- Strong Python skills and proficiency in at least one static language such as C, C++, C#, Java, or Scala.
- A proven track record deploying, monitoring, and debugging scalable AI/ML models.
- Strong electrical-engineering fundamentals, including computer architecture, high-speed interfaces, timing, power basics, and an understanding of firmware/driver structures and hardware interaction.
- Experience working within a silicon development environment, including exposure to chip and system characterization methodologies.
- Hands-on experience with silicon bring-up, characterization, or lab debug using standard tools such as oscilloscopes, multimeters, and logic analyzers.
- Ability to balance multiple simultaneous projects with strong problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills.

## Ways to Stand Out

- Familiarity with modern AI technologies and methods for creating and launching LLMs, with the ability to translate AI research into practical, high-impact production tools.
- Demonstrated experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Hands-on experience with agentic and orchestration tools such as NeMo Agent Toolkit, LangChain, Semantic Kernel, AutoGen, CrewAI, or n8n.
- Experience debugging complex system-level hardware/software interactions, including ownership of root-cause analysis for silicon- or feature-level issues.
