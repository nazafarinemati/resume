## The Role:
Architect, Analyze and optimize high-performance SoCs for Cloud computing, and machine learning acceleration. Propose solutions to enhance performance and optimize power. Explore, analyze and optimize next-generation data center systems.
## The Person:
Do you seek the most sophisticated challenges in the computing industry? As a passionate system performance architect on our design engineering team, you will propose and implement solutions to our next generation GPU SoC and data center system application performance issues.
## Key Responsibilities:
Serve as a Performance Architect in AMD's electronics design engineering team.
Participate in microarchitecture exploration, performance modeling, and analysis for next-generation GPU and data center systems.
Intimately understand design architecture and propose solutions to enhance performance 
Responsible for micro-benchmarking, workload characterization, competitive analysis, bottleneck identification, and optimization.
Develop tools and methodology for performance analysis of workloads.
Communicate, propose, and implement solutions to processor and system performance issues.
## Preferred Experience:
Experience with GPU, CPU or computer system microarchitecture
Experience with the development and usage of computer system performance models
Experience with computer workload analysis
Strong programming skills, including experience with C++ and Python (or similar)
## MINIMUM EXPERIENCE:
PhD in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or a closely related field of study + 5 years of experience.
MS in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or a closely related field of study plus 8 years of experience as a Computer Architect, Design Engineer, or in a closely related processor design role
Bachelors plus 12 years of relevant experience.
## Academic Credentials:
Bachelors, Masters, or PhD degree in Electrical Engineer, Computer Engineering, Computer Science or a closely related field
Masters or PhD with emphasis on Computer Architecture performance studies preferred
## Location:
Austin TX preferred; Santa Clara, CA
