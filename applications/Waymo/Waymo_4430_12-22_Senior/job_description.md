## About Waymo and the Perception team

Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver, The World's Most Experienced Driver, to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo's fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. It has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by over 100 million autonomous miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

The Perception team builds the system which learns the spatial-temporal representation and semantic meanings of the environment surrounding the autonomously driving vehicle. The team works jointly with downstream teams on optimization and integration into the Waymo Driver, conducts research to address real-world problems, and collaborates with research teams at Alphabet. Engineers have access to millions of miles of driving data from diverse sensors to develop efficient continual-learning methods, train models at scale, analyze real-world behavior, build systems for real-world interaction complexity, and optimize models for onboard and offboard hardware.

## You will

- Conduct research and development on novel pre-training techniques, focusing on efficiently integrating new, diverse, and multimodal data streams, such as visual data from different sensors, into existing models.
- Develop and rigorously evaluate metrics and methodologies for measuring the performance and transferability of continually pre-trained foundation models in autonomous driving.
- Stay current with advancements in large language models, vision-language models, and continual learning, and translate relevant research into production-ready systems.
