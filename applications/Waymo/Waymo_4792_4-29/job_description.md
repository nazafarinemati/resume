## About Waymo

Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo's fully autonomous ride-hail service and can be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. It has provided over ten million rider-only trips, driven over 100 million autonomous miles on public roads, and completed tens of billions of miles in simulation across more than 15 U.S. states.

## Team

The ML Optimization team provides tools that support and automate the ML workflow lifecycle, including feature and experiment management, model development, optimization, and monitoring. The team is building the next-generation onboard ML inference engine for Waymo foundation models and works across efficient models, compression, ML software such as JAX, XLA, Triton, and CUDA, and deployment under limited compute resources.

## You will

- Architect and develop an efficient, high-performance ML runtime and serving system for onboard autonomous-vehicle compute and large-scale offboard data-center environments.
- Lead integration and feature development for ML inference runtimes, balancing real-time latency and memory constraints onboard with high-throughput, highly concurrent offboard serving.
- Drive migration of ML workloads toward a JAX-native runtime architecture by extending and modifying compilers and runtimes such as OpenXLA/PjRT and TensorRT.
- Collaborate with ML practitioners across Perception, Planner, and Research to analyze system-level workloads and apply hardware-aware compute optimizations.
- Design and build robust profiling and benchmarking tools that identify system-level bottlenecks across the end-to-end ML software stack.

## You have

- BS or MS in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Deep Learning, or a related field.
- Five or more years of professional software-engineering experience building, scaling, or maintaining ML systems and infrastructure.
- Five or more years of production C++ programming.
- Three or more years of production Python and major deep-learning-framework experience, such as PyTorch or JAX.
- Experience optimizing ML software for hardware accelerators such as GPUs, TPUs, or custom silicon.
- Experience building low-latency, highly concurrent distributed backend systems.

## Preferred

- PhD in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Deep Learning, or a related field.
- Experience modifying ML compilers, runtimes, or inference engines such as TensorRT, ONNX Runtime, OpenXLA/PjRT, or TVM.
- Experience building or scaling LLM serving systems, including distributed inference and performance optimization such as KV/prefix caching and continuous batching.
- Custom-kernel development experience with CUDA, CUDA Tile, Triton, or JAX/Pallas.
- Experience architecting unified serving APIs and optimizing tensor-buffer management, including zero-copy transfer and shared memory, for complex multi-model inference pipelines.
